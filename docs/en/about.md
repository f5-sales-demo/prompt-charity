Prompt Charity combines two project ideas: useful prompts offered freely, and charitable interpretation of a user's intent.

The philosophical [principle of charity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_charity) calls for considering the strongest reasonable interpretation of a statement. For these prompts, this means clarifying ambiguity, making assumptions explicit, and preserving the user's goals and constraints. It does not mean accepting unsupported claims or replacing the user's objective with an invented one.

An internet search for the exact phrase on October 22, 2026 did not establish a recognized technical definition. The meaning above is this project's interpretation of the name. The library does not claim charitable-organization status or solicit donations.

## Our scope

Categorized use cases, English source, copyable prompts, and reviewable plans before implementation. Prompts must stay independent of harness brands, slash commands, proprietary tools, and operating-system conventions. Harness setup guidance belongs outside the copyable prompt.
