## Registration and completion

Expect 20-45 minutes for registration/upgrades, roughly 30-50 minutes overall,
with cold downloads/init sometimes longer. Budget at most 60 minutes of polling
from VM creation, including command restarts. Announce downloads, init, apply,
registration and teardown waits. Poll every 30 seconds and print each poll with
visible elapsed time. Keep the session active; use bounded tool waits and continue
collecting output without detaching the work or ending the turn while pending.

Validate HTTP status, JSON shape and a non-empty recognized spec.site_state from
/config/namespaces/system/sites/<exact-owned-site>. Distinguish transport errors,
HTTP/auth errors, invalid JSON/shape, pending states and terminal failure. Unknown
states are invalid, not success. ONLINE is the only registration success. Pending
WAITING_FOR_REGISTRATION, PROVISIONING or UPGRADING can oscillate. FAILED or
ERROR_IN_ORCHESTRATION is a failure. Bound transient retries within the original
60-minute deadline, stop promptly on authorization or repeated invalid responses,
and never return success on timeout. Preserve raw evidence privately and report
which completion gates remain unmet.

After 60 minutes stop polling and perform at most 10 minutes of read-only diagnostics:
owned VM status and serial logs, NAT mappings, effective firewall rules, site/node
responses and JWT age/validity without exposing its value. Do not retry indefinitely.
No automatic taint, token rotation, VM reset, replacement or resource deletion.
Propose a reviewed recovery plan checking state ownership, token validity and
ignored user-data: rotating a token alone will not update VM metadata, and a reset
can reuse stale data. Explain exact replacement/metadata effects and require
approval of recovery changes before executing them. Keep the failed attempt.

Completion requires evidence for ALL three gates:
1. Validated site_state=ONLINE.
2. /config/namespaces/system/securemesh_site_v2s/<site> reports exactly the expected
   node's ens4=site_local_network (SLO, primary=true) and
   ens5=site_local_inside_network (SLI, primary=false), matched to the owned VM NICs.
3. A POST-registration terraform plan -detailed-exitcode returns 0. Exit 1 is error;
   exit 2 is drift. Never apply that drift blindly or call an earlier clean plan success.

Record connected_re separately from registration; validate its response shape and
report observed RE identities/count without equating ONLINE with connectivity.
Empty sli_address is not a reliable SLI check; inspect node_list instead.
Summarize met/unmet gates, timestamps, immutable provider/image identities, total
elapsed time, cost of keeping the VM and owned teardown instructions.

## Teardown when authorized

Create and inspect a saved destroy plan for ONLY the state-owned resources, apply
that plan, and prove state is empty. Independently query exact owned GCP instance,
boot disk, networks, subnets, router/NAT, firewall rules and licence probe, plus XC
site, Secure Mesh v2 object and token. A valid absence response or validated list
without the exact object proves absence; transport errors, malformed responses or
empty unvalidated output do not. Bound XC deletion checks and preserve failures.
Do not clean by broad prefix or delete unrelated resources. Report cleanup as
incomplete until all owned resources and the XC site are independently absent.
