## Deployment requirements

**Identity and access.** Discover the actual host/platform, Terraform (at least 1.5), available tools and credential mechanisms. Verify caller and effective GCP identity, impersonation access when selected, deployment permissions, required APIs/quota-project access, regional capacity and both CLI and Terraform ADC authentication. Check project, image and existing network inventories as the selected deployment identity; do not fall back to the human caller when impersonation fails. Verify XC create/delete access and private GitHub release access independently.

CLI login alone does not establish Terraform identity. For impersonation, use exactly one method: existing impersonated ADC, or primary-user ADC with provider/environment impersonation. Resolve conflicting explicit credentials within the task environment. Privately verify short-lived token identity and expiration; request cloud-platform and userinfo.email scopes when needed to verify email. Existing organization-required JSON keys stay outside Terraform inputs; create no new keys. Keep customer-owned accounts, bindings and credentials after teardown.

A validated permission response missing a required permission is blocked. Generic 403s, unavailable policies, malformed responses and transport errors remain unknown; visible IAM bindings alone do not prove effective access. Token Creator must authorize the caller on the selected service account (or its owning project); a grant to the service account itself does not authorize the caller. Token Creator, deployment rights and actAs for an attached VM identity are different permissions. Preserve approved custom roles and avoid broad Owner/Editor grants.

When blocked, prepare one private administrator handoff identifying caller, target service account/owning project, deployment/quota projects, missing verified permission, sanitized evidence/time and recheck action. Request investigation for unknown failures rather than guessing a grant. Keep fixture or customer administration outside CE deployment authority: create no accounts, grant no IAM roles, enable no APIs and change no organization policy under deployment approval. Following administrator confirmation, recheck effective access about every 30 seconds for at most ten minutes from that original confirmation, including resumes. Timeout preserves uncertainty and requires review; it never resets the budget or repeats an IAM mutation.

**Provider and image.** Discover the newest non-draft release, including prereleases, from the private repository https://github.com/F5Networks/terraform-provider-f5xc-preview. Authenticate access; a private-repository 404 may mean missing permission. Verify the native platform archive against its published SHA256 checksum, inspect its schema and pin the exact discovered version before initialization. Retain archive/binary identity and lockfile evidence; do not silently reuse an older cache. Existing deployments require a reviewed provider upgrade.

Select an approved READY f5xc-ce-crt BYOL image from f5-7626-networks-public, recording exact name, immutable ID, creation time, minimum disk size and guest features. Verify Secure Mesh v2 compatibility against the current GCP deployment guide and selected provider/API schema. Metadata readability does not prove image-use or marketplace licence capability. If only this capability remains uncertain, include a narrowly scoped temporary disk probe from the pinned image in the approved scope, using the selected identity and sufficient capacity. Verify its deletion independently; licence acceptance belongs to the customer. Do not invent publisher IAM changes.

**Network and compute.** Use one n2-standard-8 VM without an external IP, with separate new custom-mode SLO and SLI networks and non-overlapping private /26 subnets. Check all existing primary and secondary ranges. The outside interface ens4 is SLO and primary; ens5 is SLI and nonprimary. Provide Cloud Router/NAT on SLO for required outbound access. Scope firewall rules to the owned CE and validate effective inherited policy; do not change existing networks or bypass organizational restrictions.

Derive current Secure Mesh v2 registration, Regional Edge, software-download, DNS and NTP destinations and protocols from https://docs.cloud.f5.com/docs-v2/multi-cloud-network-connect/reference/ce-ip-dom-ref. Preserve its TCP/UDP distinctions rather than unioning destination sets or opening unrestricted tunnels. Allow required registration HTTPS, RE TCP 80/443 and UDP 4500/123, software HTTPS and approved DNS/NTP access as the current reference requires. Review broad CDN HTTPS implications. CE-to-CE mesh is excluded: no UDP 500 or ESP. Default ingress is none; optional SSH requires an approved key, narrow TCP 22 sources and an actual private connectivity path.

Verify current machine/storage requirements at https://docs.cloud.f5.com/docs-v2/multi-cloud-network-connect/reference/ce-site-size-ref and the GCP workflow at https://docs.cloud.f5.com/docs-v2/multi-cloud-network-connect/how-to/site-management/deploy-sms-gcp-clickops. The 120 GB pd-balanced default reserves 50% above an 80 GB minimum; increase if current requirements or the image demand it and include cost/quota review. Provisioned disk capacity does not prove guest-usable upgrade headroom. Respect image boot compatibility: omit incompatible Shielded VM/UEFI settings for non-UEFI images. Do not guess filesystem expansion commands.

**Site and bootstrap.** Use f5xc_securemesh_site_v2 in system with GCP not-managed orchestration, single-node HA disabled, blocked services, logs streaming disabled, F5 default DNS/NTP and geographic RE selection. Verify exact one-of attribute syntax and semantics against the selected schema. Send no GCP credential key to XC. Let registration populate node_list and discovered interfaces.

Preserve registration-managed provider_choice, labels, operating_system_version and volterra_software_version through appropriate lifecycle exclusions, and verify their live values independently because exclusions hide drift. Use f5xc_token with JWT type bound to the site; pass its content string, not its ID or an indexed character. Mark JWT interpolation and metadata sensitive, never output the token, and privately inspect actual iat/exp rather than assuming a fixed lifetime.

Secure Mesh v2 cloud-init writes token: <JWT> to /etc/vpm/user_data, root-owned with mode 0600. Use no SMSv1 config, Vpm/Kubernetes nesting or registration_approval resource. Verify bootstrap metadata privately. Account for ignored metadata during recovery: token rotation alone may not update the VM, and a reset may reuse expired data. Collect supported read-only guest disk/partition/filesystem and free-space diagnostics for root and persistent data/log paths without exposing bootstrap credentials.

## Acceptance criteria

Report completion only with evidence for every applicable gate, separating Terraform creation from registration, connectivity and guest storage:

- Reviewed configuration and saved-plan scope match the approved architecture, identities, provider/image pins and exact owned inventory; Terraform formatting and validation succeed. Retain reviewable code and private state and plan evidence in the chosen workspace or approved backend.
- Within 60 minutes of original VM creation, obtain two consecutive validated site_state=ONLINE observations at least 30 seconds apart. Poll about every 30 seconds with elapsed time; check the original deadline before requests and accepting responses. Pending, malformed, failed or timed-out responses cannot pass.
- Independently verify the expected node's ens4 SLO primary and ens5 SLI nonprimary roles against owned VM NICs, including MAC/device evidence. The Secure Mesh v2 API reports these under spec.gcp.not_managed.node_list[].interface_list[], with network_option and is_primary; an empty sli_address is insufficient.
- After registration and interface verification, terraform plan -detailed-exitcode returns zero, followed by another validated ONLINE observation. Exit one is error and two is drift. Review drift before changing anything. A regression requires renewed stability, interfaces and plan evidence within the original deadline.
- Record Regional Edge connectivity separately. After registration and any bootstrap upgrade reboot, independently verify approved boot-disk type/capacity and supported guest-usable filesystem/free-space evidence for root and persistent data/log paths. Ensure the chosen diagnostic route remains available then; first-boot measurements alone do not establish the post-registration storage gate. Missing guest evidence remains unknown; provisioning alone never proves an upgrade test.

At registration timeout, preserve the attempt and allow at most ten minutes of read-only diagnostics of the owned VM, serial output, NAT, effective firewalls, site/node responses and token validity. Recovery mutations, replacement, reset, token rotation and deletion require a reviewed scope and plan, with exact metadata/ownership consequences. Avoid indefinite retries.

When teardown is authorized, inspect and apply the saved destroy plan for owned resources only. Independently prove absence of the exact VM, boot disk, VPCs, subnets, router/NAT, firewall rules, temporary probes, XC site, Secure Mesh v2 representation and registration token, and verify empty owned state. Use the registration-token inventory at /register/namespaces/system/tokens. Validated absence or complete validated inventories can pass; transport failures and unvalidated empty output cannot. Never delete by broad prefix or remove customer-owned IAM/credentials. Reconcile interrupted operations before a new reviewed destroy plan. Report unmet gates, timestamps, remaining costs and exact remaining cleanup obligations without exposing private evidence.
